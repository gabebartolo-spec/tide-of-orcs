# Devlog — M1: the arcing bow

The second playable build of Tide of Orcs is up on the dev channel.

## What's new
- **A new way to shoot.** Move your pointer over the plain and a strike circle follows it. Every 2.5 seconds the elf lobs one arrow in a high arc. It lands on the circle and hurts every orc inside. If you don't aim, she doesn't shoot. It's an incremental, not an idle. Your companions and vanguard still fight on their own.
- **Wide Volley.** A new node in the tree: each rank makes the circle 20% wider, up to 5 ranks.
- **Old nodes, new tricks.** Split Shot drops extra arrows inside the circle. Longshaft strikes the same spot again. Moonbow snaps the circle to the nearest orc and hits harder. Quick Draw shortens the wait between arrows.
- **Tree pan and zoom,** a cleaner first run, autosave, and old saves carried over safely.

## What to test
- Does aiming feel good? Is 2.5 seconds between arrows too slow, or just right?
- How far does your first run get before the wall falls, and how much further does the second one go?
- On phones, the Replant button can be cut off at the bottom of the field. That's a known issue and the fix is coming.
