# Devlog 16 — A lit world, 100 achievements and cloak dyes

*Build: `html5-dev` from `main` (d428c06), 13 Nov 2026 (itch run 43399625004). Covers: everything since devlog 15 (69ce8bf). Posted: https://veniceissinking.itch.io/elves-vs-orcs/devlog/1774433*

This build relights the whole field under one sun, and adds achievements with the first cosmetic rewards.

## What's new
- **One sun over everything.** The elf, orcs, wolves, vanguard and the barricade are now rendered lit, with cast shadows. The ground is a painted bake under the same sun, and the treeline is lit in depth rows.
- **The sky follows the day.** It moves from midday to golden hour to dusk as the run goes on.
- **The barricade splinters.** Damage now shows as splintered timber; the base never opens up.
- **Better movement.** Orcs jog and vanguard stride with feet that match the ground speed; wolves gallop and move a little faster (speed 105).
- **Lit hits and deaths.** Hit and death animations match the new lighting.
- **HUD.** The HUD is lit by the same sun, with a carved name tag and a wave strip.
- **Achievements.** 100 achievements across five groups. A toast appears when you earn one, and the Achievements tab (between runs) lists them all. Many give a title you can wear.
- **Cloak dyes.** Five cloak and hood dyes for the elf: ember (wave 50), frost (tier 3), dusk (tier 5), starlit (tier 6) and gold (wave 200). Wear one from its row in the Achievements tab. The other cosmetic rewards arrive with later art passes.
