# Devlog — 1 Nov: loot, a new name and a living-wood grove

*Itch devlog for the html5-dev build pushed from `main` on 1 Nov 2026 (itch run 41288928458). It covers everything since the `m1` build (53b391d, 30 Oct).*

The game has a new name: **Let's Hunt Some Orc!** This build is the biggest one yet: the full companion armoury, prestige, loot, a new look and real art and sound.

## What's new
- **Loot.** Orcs now drop gear. You wear it in six slots, and each piece carries rolled affixes. There are seven sets with 2, 4 and 6-piece bonuses that show on the field, and eight mythic items, each with its own power. Look in the Satchel tab to wear, lock or salvage items. Salvaging pays Glimmer, which rerolls one affix on a worn item. Every item has its own icon.
- **Bosses.** A boss wave unlocks every 50 waves. Bosses are the best source of rare drops.
- **The full armoury.** Wolf, ranger, hawk and treant fight beside you. You also get knives, fire, storm and the ballista, and each one has its own wind-up and hit effect.
- **Six new arrows.** Rebound, Starfall, Frost, Shatter, Shock and Thunderclap. Each has its own colour, shape and sound, and burning or chilled orcs glow on their own bodies.
- **More orcs.** Runners, brutes and blackguards now join the grunts, alongside spearmen and bladedancers.
- **Replant and the Heartwood tree.** When the wall falls, the grove burns and a seed drops. Heartwood buys permanent upgrades, including six that improve loot, and a run summary shows how far you got. The skill tree is now bought between runs. From wave 12 you can choose to Replant early.
- **A much bigger tree.** There are now over a hundred nodes, including upgrades for each unit type, a Spoils branch for loot, a branch legend, keyboard controls and a line that tells you what each node needs first.
- **A new look.** The game has its own elf typeface. The interface is carved living wood on forest green, and the field fills the window. Orcs and the elf are moving from vector shapes to animated sprites: grunts, spearmen and bladedancers are in, with more to come. The grove has hills, a day-to-dusk sky and worn ground.
- **Sound.** Sixteen sound effects, an ambient forest bed and two music beds that duck when the wall falls or a boss arrives.
- **Fixes.** A wall that could be one-shot, a combat stall in very late waves, and the Replant button that phones could not reach. The aim circle is now 20 px, so aiming is tighter.

## What to test
- Does loot feel rewarding? How long until your first rare, epic and legendary item?
- Does buying the tree between runs feel better or worse than buying mid-run?
- How far does each run get before the wall falls, and does each Replant feel faster than the last?
- Is the new text easy to read at your screen size?
