# Devlog 5 — 2 Nov (night): a standing line, a mixed tide, checkpoints

*Build: `html5-dev` from `main` (ba521f8), 2 Nov 2026 (itch run 41436431610). Covers: everything since the 2 Nov build (1346a52). Posted: https://veniceissinking.itch.io/elves-vs-orcs/devlog/1747072*

This build answers the last playtest: the vanguard felt useless, the tide was all grunts, and long runs dragged back to wave 1.

## What's new
- **A standing vanguard line.** Spearmen and bladedancers hold the wall and come back on a timer when they fall (Swift Muster makes it shorter). They walk out only to orcs that have crossed halfway, then return and face the field. The rally buttons are gone.
- **A mixed tide.** Goblin runners now join on their own from wave 5, brutes from wave 10 and the Blackguard from wave 20, at a trickle. Their summon nodes now make that kind march four times as often, each worth +50% starlight.
- **Checkpoints.** Clear wave 10, 20, 30 … and every later run starts one wave past it. Existing saves get the checkpoint for the best wave they reached.
- **A cleaner field.** Hand-drawn meadow flowers, drifting motes and sun shafts replace the 3D plants. Every actor now draws from its sprite; the old placeholder shapes are gone.
- **Wolf tuning.** The Grey Wolf costs 200 and bites for 4, so it no longer carries the early game alone.

## What to test
- Does the standing line feel useful, and do units come back quickly enough?
- Wave 5 to 12: do runners feel fair, or do they break the wall too fast?
- Do checkpoints make a new run feel like progress, or does starting at wave 11 feel too hard?
- Is anything on the field missing or drawn wrong now that the placeholder shapes are gone?
